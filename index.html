THE PROCESS

How Books Are Judged

Every submitted book is carefully evaluated against a clear and consistent standard. Recognition is earned through the quality, originality, presentation and overall impact of the work.

A Fair Process For
Exceptional Books

01

Assessment

Every submission is carefully assessed across the published judging criteria, giving each book a consistent and thoughtful evaluation.

02

Professional Review

The assessment is reviewed in the context of the book's category, considering its purpose, audience and overall literary qualities.

03

Final Determination

The completed evaluation is considered against the standard before the final recognition decision is made.

Excellence Deserves Recognition

Our judging framework considers the complete reading experience — from the strength of the writing and originality of the work to its reader impact, presentation and category relevance.
