More Than One Fit?

Some Books Cross Boundaries.

A book may genuinely belong in more than one category. A personal story about building a company may potentially belong in:

  • Memoir
  • Business
  • Entrepreneurship

The correct choice depends on what the book is primarily doing for its reader.
